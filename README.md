Entry for make.fun's bounty "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" (gta6-meme-x-01), entered as @tapetelo.

The entry is the post itself: https://x.com/0x8papat/status/2123447365080416695

The meme: a hero duo in a pink convertible at Vice City sunset, make.fun's ghost
mascot driving with its magenta star coin, five wanted stars overhead —
"Called in sick for GTA 6. 5-star wanted at work. My agent on make.fun paid the fine."

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them in
paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.

`POST.json` names the post; `main.ts` copies it to the output directory for the
grader. Run locally with:

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
